Optional snack

16:00, any day if hungry: a few hard-boiled eggs, or a small handful of amandes / noix de macadamia (lowest-carb nuts).

Practical notes

  • Salt generously — without carbs, you lose sodium faster than usual
  • Ease training intensity these 5 days — expect strength to dip slightly, that's the glycogen effect, not something going wrong
  • Cook everything in butter — it's your primary fat source here, adding calories and flavor without carbs
  • Protein should land naturally around 150–180g/day without much extra effort
  • Lardons or poitrine fumée are a good way to add fat and flavor to eggs or sautéed veg if you want variety beyond straight butter

Moving into the main plan

After day 5, the shift into the full 7-day plan will feel like adding carbs back in, not starting from scratch — a smoother transition than jumping straight into training with a full macro shift on day one.
