The Advisory Visit Guide

How to get your security deposit back when leaving!

When you are a tenant and preparing to move out, the main question is how to maximise your chances of getting your security deposit back in full. The golden rule is: returning the property in a state similar to the one noted in your check-in inventory will allow you to recover your deposit.

Top 11 Tips for Preparing Your Property

Tip 1: Deep Cleaning
Do a thorough clean of your home. Do not forget to scrub the tile grout and clean the windows (on both the inside and outside).
Tip 2: Taps and Showers
Mixer taps must work normally, and showerheads must have a regular spray. If you replace the showerhead, ensure it fits the wall bracket. The shower hose must be in good condition and not leak.
Tip 3: Plugs and Drains
Plugs in sinks, bathtubs, and washbasins must hold water. Check that the traps (U-bends) underneath do not leak, and remember to empty and unblock them.
Tip 4: Silicone Sealant
The waterproof seals around the kitchen worktop, electric hobs, bathroom sinks, and shower/bath trays must be clean and in good condition to prevent water damage.
Tip 5: Descaling
If taps are dripping, washers/seals need changing. Ensure taps and aerators are not clogged with limescale.
DIY Descaling Tip: Soak them for 2 days in half a glass of vinegar mixed with two tablespoons of salt and a little lemon juice. Sinks must be completely free of limescale.
Tip 6: Toilets
Check that the flush works and does not leak. The toilet seat and lid must be in good condition. If the enamel is chipped, there are special repair products available. Descale the bowl.
Tip 7: Walls and Ceilings
Fill any holes neatly with smooth filler after removing wall plugs, and sand it down once dry. Wash marked walls, or repaint the affected section. Remove mould with diluted bleach.
Tip: "Magic erasers" sold in DIY stores are highly effective for removing marks. (Note: If you suffered water damage, you must have declared it to your insurance).
Tip 8: Keys and Locks
You must return the exact same number of keys, fobs, and access badges given to you at the start of your tenancy. Locks must be in good working order—lubricate them regularly.
Tip 9: Windows and Shutters
If a window pane is broken, remember this is usually covered by your home insurance. Window and door latches must work perfectly. Roller shutters must go up and down without squeaking, and the slats must let daylight through when closed.
Tip 10: Electrics
Light fittings, doorbells, and radiators must work. Check sockets and switches. Ensure there is a working lightbulb in every room. (Note: exposed wiring blocks/"dominoes" are no longer permitted due to safety standards; leave a ceiling rose/fitting in place if unsure).
Tip 11: Gardens and Exteriors
Lawns must be mowed, flowerbeds weeded, trees/shrubs pruned, and fences maintained. Outdoor taps and sockets must work. Remove moss from patios and unblock gutters/drains. Grease gate and garage hinges/locks. Empty the septic tank if you have one.

Important Administrative & General Information

  • Watch out for damage: Scratches on wooden floors, chipped tiles, burnt/damaged kitchen worktops, and marks on doors/walls from moving furniture can result in deductions from your deposit.
  • Utilities: Remember to cancel or transfer your electricity, telephone, and internet accounts, and set up mail forwarding.
  • What happens when you leave? The check-out inventory determines if the letting agent or landlord will deduct money from your deposit to cover repair quotes.
  • Service Charges: The landlord will reconcile the service charges for the current year. By law, they can withhold up to 20% of your deposit pending receipt of the final utility/building management bills to calculate this regularisation.

Final Check-List Breakdown

General & Extras

  • Reattach any loose skirting boards, sockets, and switch covers.
  • Sweep and empty the garage completely.
  • Ensure sliding wardrobe doors are correctly on their tracks (replace wheels/clips if needed).
  • Clean all air vents and extractor fans.
  • Heating: Dust electric radiators. If you have a gas boiler, you must provide the annual maintenance certificate (otherwise a full service will be charged to you). Gas hoses must be in-date. Chimneys require an annual sweeping certificate.
  • Remove any personal modifications (shelves, custom wallpaper, sticky hooks, nails) unless agreed otherwise.

Kitchen

  • Descale tiles; clean cupboards inside and out (remove any shelf paper).
  • Fridge: Defrost the freezer, wash all shelves and drawers with soapy water, dry them, and leave the doors slightly open. Ensure all original parts (ice cube trays, egg holders) are present and unbroken.
  • Oven/Hob/Extractor: Degrease everything. Replace the extractor hood grease filter. Clean glass-ceramic hobs with specialist products. Ensure oven trays and grills are clean.
  • Dishwasher: Clean the filters, door edges, and rubber seals.

Bathroom & Toilets

  • Descale everything (shower screens, tiles, taps).
  • Clean the toilet thoroughly, including under the rim.
  • Wash the mirror and inside/outside of the bathroom cabinet.
  • Replace the showerhead or hose if they are defective or heavily calcified.
  • Ensure plugholes and overflows are clear of hair and debris.

Floors & Windows

  • Wash windows on all four sides (inside and out) and leave them open briefly to dry and prevent condensation.
  • Shampoo carpets (use a specialist extraction machine).
  • Clean wooden floors with a damp mop and mild soap.
  • Remove limescale from tiled floors using cleaning vinegar.

Exteriors

  • Clean moss off balconies/terraces.
  • Remove any oil stains from the garage floor or parking space.
  • Clean your letterbox, remove your name tag, and remove any "no junk mail" stickers.
