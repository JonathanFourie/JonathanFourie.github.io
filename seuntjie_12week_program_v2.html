Progression & Key Principles
Overload
Each week, do one of: add 1 rep per set, reduce rest by 10s, or increase weight by one KB size. Never change more than one variable at once.
Skill session
Phase 1 (Wk 3–4): 2 warm-up rounds, 6–8 reps, 12 kg. Phase 2: 3 rounds, 10 reps, 12–16 kg. Phase 3: full benchmark, 16 kg swings/rows, 12–16 kg windmills/press.
Equipment order
Single 24 kg (buy now) → Single 28 kg (~Wk 8) → Steel mace 7 kg (after Phase 1) → Second 24 kg (~Mo 4–5) → Single 32 kg (~Mo 5–6).
Visceral fat
Responds to: (1) heavy compound strength work, (2) a 300–400 kcal daily deficit, (3) 7–8 hours sleep. Target 2,000–2,100 kcal/day with 180–200g protein.
Warm-up
5 min every session: 10 hip circles each direction, 10 leg swings each leg, 10 arm circles, 10 bodyweight squats, 10 hip hinges.
Cool-down
5 min: hip flexor stretch, hamstring stretch, thoracic rotation, child's pose. Phase 3: add a 5-min walk after each session.
Tracking
Weigh on RENPHO every 2 weeks, same time of day. Visceral fat score is the primary metric — body weight fluctuates, visceral fat does not lie.

The one thing that matters
Show up, log the set, chase the benchmark.
Everything in this program funnels toward one session — Workout S. Three rounds of warm-up, ten reps a side, four lifts, clean. When you hit it twice in a row, the kettlebell gets heavier and the program moves with you.
