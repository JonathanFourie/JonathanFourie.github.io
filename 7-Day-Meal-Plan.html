Closing the gap to 170g

Most days land around 118–135g from the core meals — that's expected, since exact protein varies by cut and cooking method. Keep one of these on hand to top up without overthinking it:

  • Skyr or 0% fromage blanc, 200g tub≈20g · 120 kcal
  • Two boiled eggs, fridge-ready≈13g · 140 kcal
  • Whey shake, mixed with water≈25–30g · 120–150 kcal

Shopping list

Protein

Eggs, canned tuna, chicken breast, chicken thighs, bavette / faux-filet, cabillaud, saumon, blanc de dinde, skyr, fromage blanc 0%

Carbs & veg

Potatoes, quinoa, Puy lentils, chickpeas, sweet potato, green beans, courgette, peppers, carrots, mixed greens, tomatoes

Fats & extras

Olive oil, almonds, walnuts, tahini, comté (small amount), olives, honey

One more thing

This plan supports muscle retention and growth, but the actual signal to build muscle comes from resistance training. Aim for 3–4 sessions a week hitting the major compound lifts — squat, deadlift, press, row — to make full use of the protein you're eating.
